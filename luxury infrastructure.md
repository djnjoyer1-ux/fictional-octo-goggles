# What would the most expensive possible 3-day trip to Mongolia from London actually look like?

This is purely hypothetical, but I was wondering how far you could realistically push the budget for a **3-day trip to Mongolia** if money was essentially no object.

Here's what I came up with:

- Charter a **Bombardier Global 7500** or **Gulfstream G700** from London (**£80k–140k** round trip).
- VIP handling on arrival in Ulaanbaatar, then transfer straight to the Gobi by private helicopter.
- Stay at **Three Camel Lodge**, or go a step further and have a completely private luxury expedition camp built exclusively for your group.
- Spend the next day flying between the Gobi's highlights by helicopter:
  - Khongoryn Els (Singing Dunes)
  - Flaming Cliffs
  - Private champagne breakfast in the desert
  - Gourmet lunch by a private chef
  - Falconry and eagle hunter demonstrations
  - Stargazing with an astronomer that evening.
- On the final day, helicopter to Terelj National Park for horseback riding, luxury picnic, and a private cultural experience with nomadic families before flying back to London on the jet.

If you really wanted to go overboard, you could also add:
- Charter the entire lodge.
- Fly in a Michelin-starred chef from Europe.
- Bring a sommelier with rare Bordeaux and Burgundy.
- Hire a National Geographic-style photographer and film crew.
- Personal security team.
- Medical concierge.
- Luxury expedition vehicles as backup to the helicopters.
- Live traditional Mongolian musicians for a private performance.

### Rough cost estimate

- **Private jet:** £80k–140k
- **Helicopters:** £15k–30k
- **Accommodation:** £5k–20k
- **Bespoke experiences & logistics:** £20k–50k+

**Estimated total:** **£120k–250k**, with **£300k+** definitely possible if you start chartering entire properties and bringing international staff.

I'm curious if anyone here has actually done ultra-luxury travel in Mongolia. Are there experiences even more exclusive than this, or does the country's limited luxury infrastructure mean you eventually hit a ceiling where spending more just means importing your own luxury?
